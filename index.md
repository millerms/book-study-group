# Buddhist Bible Study
Hey friends - Here is a light structure to understand Buddhism a little more systematically: where the different traditions came from, how they relate to one another, and what some of the early texts actually say.

So this is our shared reading notebook and nothing here is set in stone.

We can change the schedule, skip things, spend longer on something interesting, or add totally different readings as we go. The main point is just to have one place where we can see what we're reading next and find the links without digging through texts and group chats.

## Rough reading plan

I put together a tentative 12-week path through early Buddhism, Theravada, Mahayana, East Asian Buddhism, Vajrayana, and some of the ways Buddhism developed in the modern world.
